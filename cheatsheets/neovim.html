Good to know your settings
Leader is Space — mirrors the tmux Ctrl-Space prefix, so prefix X in tmux and Spc X here match.
System clipboard — yank, delete, and put use the OS clipboard; no register prefix needed.
Smart search case — case-insensitive until you type a capital, then case-sensitive.
Persistent undo — undo/redo history survives closing and reopening a file.
Hybrid line numbers — absolute on the cursor line, relative everywhere else.
2-space indent — tabs expand to spaces; new splits open right and below.
